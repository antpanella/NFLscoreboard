Layout

The spotlit game grows to four times its size where it sits. Big moments are a close finish (the Alerts tab's close-game rule, or overtime) and a red-zone trip in a game where you have a fantasy starter or a live bet. Click any card to pin it there; click it again, or press Esc, to let go. Clicking a big moment waves it off until it is over. Needs three or more live games.

Before the first kick the board shows a countdown and the friends’ bet code, with these panels taking turns underneath. A bet landing while it is up takes the screen for a few seconds.

Your phone

One code for you: every setting, delay, mute, spotlight, the lights' stop button, and your bets — with a name field so your bets carry your name. It carries your token, so it only shows while this is ticked and Settings is open.

Friends' bets

One code for friends: bets only. Their bets show on the board with their name, and they can only add or remove their own — no settings, no lights.

Relay

This token was published in an earlier version of the board. Set a new ACCESS_TOKEN in Cloudflare, then paste the new one here.

Govee blocks browsers directly, so this needs a relay: your Cloudflare Worker. The token is stored in this browser only — it is never part of the page — so enter it once on each laptop that should drive the lights. The board never sees your Govee key.

After the show

Record & replay

The last four days are kept in this browser. Export a day to send it over when something on the board needs checking.

Unattended

When no game is on, the board checks every half hour for a newer copy of itself and reloads. Never mid-game. It also restarts the score feed if it ever stalls, and asks the laptop to keep the screen on.

Tools

Resets how the board looks and alerts. Keeps your Sleeper account, your lights, your bets and your recordings.

Backup

Moves your whole setup to another laptop in one step: Sleeper account, look, alerts, lights and bets. Recordings have their own save above. The relay token stays out of the file unless you tick it — if you do, keep that file private.
